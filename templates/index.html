Our Mission

ESP Safety saves lives and preserves capital resources in hazardous environments. We provide top-of-the-line gas and flame detectors that can be counted on in the event of an emergency. The latest technology and advanced signal processing algorithms in our products ensure no gas leak or fire will go undetected, notifying you of an event within seconds. Our field support and our unending commitment to customer service protects the investments our customers make in their people and their infrastructure.

ESP Product Certifications

ESP Safety’s line of gas detectors and flame detection products adhere to the highest industry standards. We carry certification and approvals from several global agencies like FM Approval, VDS, the American Bureau of Shipping, Nemko, TUV, Exida and many others.

About ESP Safety

Founded in 1973, ESP Safety has grown into a leading fire and gas detection company providing products and services to some of the largest global oil and gas producers in the world. Our corporate headquarters and Technology and Training Center is located in San Jose, California.
