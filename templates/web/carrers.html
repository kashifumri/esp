Join Our Team

What does ESP stand for? Some say Extra Smart People work here, other ESPians believe our motto is Enthusiastically Solving Problems.

For over fifty years ESP Safety has been protecting our customers with a full line of flame and gas detectors. We are committed to using cutting edge technology to make the world a safer place as we continue address the industry challenges of today and the future. With more than 250 employees worldwide, each location operates independently.

We believe a healthy work life balance is a high priority, but when the need arises we always do what it takes to make it happen. Our culture is based on a collaborative approach and we are committed to equality in employment opportunity. As a global company, we wholeheartedly embrace the cultural diversity within the communities we call home. When you join us you will see we are a diverse group that maintains a sense of humor and accepts everyone for who they are while working with enthusiasm, commitment, and integrity.

Join one of our teams in San Jose, CA or Houston, TX to be a part of something bigger.
